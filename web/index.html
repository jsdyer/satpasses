How these predictions work

SGP4 projects each orbit from a dated set of CelesTrak elements. A pass is the time the satellite clears your selected elevation. A likely visible window requires sunlight on the satellite and the Sun at least 6° below your horizon. Clouds, nearby obstructions, equipment and satellite brightness can affect sighting.

Pass start and end times are estimated to within a second by the threshold calculation. Illumination windows and peak elevations use 5-second samples. Real orbital error can be larger, especially after an orbit change or with old data.
